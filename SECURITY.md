# Security policy

We welcome reports of security vulnerabilities in SmartRemit.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **support@smartremit.ai** with the subject line **"Security report"**.

Please do not open a public GitHub issue, discussion or pull request for a security problem.

## What to include

- A description of the issue and its impact.
- The steps to reproduce it, including the URLs or API operations involved.
- Any proof-of-concept request or output (redact anything that is not yours).
- How we can contact you for follow-up questions.

## Scope

In scope:

- The smartremit.ai website and the pages it serves.
- The SmartRemit Partner API.

Out of scope:

- Third-party services we use (report those to the vendor directly).
- Findings that need physical access to a device, or a compromised device or account.
- Denial-of-service, volumetric or load testing.
- Social engineering of our staff, partners or customers.

## Rules of engagement

- Test only with your own sandbox keys and your own data.
- Never use live keys, and never access, change or delete data that is not yours.
- Do not send messages to phone numbers you do not own.
- Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it.

## Our commitment

We review every report, will acknowledge yours and keep you updated as we work on it.

## Bug bounty

There is no bug bounty programme today.

## Safe harbour (draft, pending review by counsel)

We will not pursue good-faith security research that follows this policy.
