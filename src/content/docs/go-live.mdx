## Before live keys are issued

Work through this list with your test key and a reference-rail test run arranged with SmartRemit. When every item is done, ask SmartRemit to take you live; SmartRemit issues your live keys.

### WhatsApp

- [ ] Your WhatsApp Business number's credentials are saved in your dashboard.
- [ ] Meta's webhook points at the callback URL on your dashboard's partner page, and the verification succeeded.
- [ ] Your message templates are approved, at minimum `transfer_delivered`. See <GuideLink slug="whatsapp-setup">WhatsApp setup</GuideLink>.

### API

- [ ] You hold a test key and call the API from your server only.
- [ ] You created, confirmed and read back a sandbox transaction end to end. See <GuideLink slug="sandbox">Sandbox</GuideLink>.
- [ ] Every `POST /transactions` sends a fresh `Idempotency-Key`, and your retries reuse it. See <GuideLink slug="idempotency">Idempotency</GuideLink>.
- [ ] You handle `429` by waiting for `Retry-After`, and `503` by retrying later. See <GuideLink slug="rate-limits">Rate limits</GuideLink> and <GuideLink slug="errors">Errors</GuideLink>.

### Settlement

- [ ] Your settlement endpoint meets the endpoint rules and is saved with its signing secret.
- [ ] Your endpoint verifies `x-smartremit-signature`, acknowledges quickly and deduplicates on `reference`.
- [ ] Your status callbacks are signed with your webhook secret and echo the amount on `paid_out`.
- [ ] In a reference-rail test run arranged with SmartRemit, you completed the full loop, including the failure path. See <GuideLink slug="sandbox">Sandbox</GuideLink> and <GuideLink slug="webhooks">Webhooks</GuideLink>.

### Compliance

- [ ] Your KYC mode is agreed, and you send `sender.kyc_status` and `sender.name` on every transaction. See <GuideLink slug="kyc-delegation">KYC delegation</GuideLink>.

### Live money on your own rail

Going live with your own settlement rail needs a second SmartRemit approval after go-live. SmartRemit approves your workspace for live money on your rail when:

- your compliance setup is signed on the Onboarding page of your workspace (signing it turns on verify before send for your customers);
- two of your team are named as compliance users, each with two-step verification;
- your own WhatsApp Business number is connected;
- your agreement with SmartRemit is on file;
- the further checks SmartRemit adds are met (your Controls page lists every check and its state).

Until then, live transactions on your rail are refused with `403` and `code` `partner_not_live`. Sandbox transactions are not affected. After the approval, a live Partner API transaction with a USDC payout is refused with `403` and `code` `capability_off` until that feature is approved. Business payments, repeat payments and AI apps stay closed in the chat, the portal, the pay pages and AI apps until they are approved, and customers see a neutral message there. SmartRemit reviews each feature first; your admin then approves it on the Controls page. See <GuideLink slug="errors">Errors</GuideLink>.

### Your switches

Your admins can pause your own workspace on the Controls page: new sends and settlement (for every corridor or for one), the AI agent and AI agent tools. A pause takes effect within about 15 seconds and stays until your team resumes it. Resuming asks the admin to confirm their identity again. SmartRemit sees each change, and your workspace shows a notice while a pause is on.
