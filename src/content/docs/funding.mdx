## Coming soon

Collecting sender funds through your own account is not enabled yet. This guide will describe it when it is available.

Until then, you collect funds on your side and tell SmartRemit a transfer is paid in one of two ways:

- confirm it through the API with `POST /transactions/{id}/confirm`, or
- report `funded` in a signed status callback (see <GuideLink slug="webhooks">Webhooks</GuideLink>).
