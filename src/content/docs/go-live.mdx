## Before live keys are issued

Work through this list with a test key. When every item is done, ask SmartRemit to take you live; SmartRemit issues your live keys.

### WhatsApp

- [ ] Your WhatsApp Business number's credentials are saved in your dashboard.
- [ ] Meta's webhook points at the callback URL on your dashboard's partner page, and the verification succeeded.
- [ ] Your message templates are approved, at minimum `transfer_delivered`. See <GuideLink slug="whatsapp-setup">WhatsApp setup</GuideLink>.

### API

- [ ] You hold a test key and call the API from your server only.
- [ ] You created, confirmed and read back a sandbox transaction end to end. See <GuideLink slug="sandbox">Sandbox</GuideLink>.
- [ ] Every `POST /transactions` sends a fresh `Idempotency-Key`, and your retries reuse it. See <GuideLink slug="idempotency">Idempotency</GuideLink>.
- [ ] You handle `429` by waiting for `Retry-After`, and `503` by retrying later. See <GuideLink slug="rate-limits">Rate limits</GuideLink> and <GuideLink slug="errors">Errors</GuideLink>.

### Settlement

- [ ] Your settlement endpoint meets the endpoint rules and is saved with its signing secret.
- [ ] Your endpoint verifies `x-smartremit-signature`, acknowledges quickly and deduplicates on `reference`.
- [ ] Your status callbacks are signed with your webhook secret and echo the amount on `paid_out`.
- [ ] You have run the full loop on the reference rail, including the failure path. See <GuideLink slug="webhooks">Webhooks</GuideLink>.

### Compliance

- [ ] Your KYC mode is agreed, and you send `sender.kyc_status` and `sender.name` on every transaction. See <GuideLink slug="kyc-delegation">KYC delegation</GuideLink>.
