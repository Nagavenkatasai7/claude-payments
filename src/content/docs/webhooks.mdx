## Two directions

- **Settlement instructions (us to you).** When a transfer is paid, SmartRemit sends a signed instruction to your settlement endpoint telling your rail to pay out.
- **Status callbacks (you to us).** Your rail reports the transfer's progress back to SmartRemit, signed with your webhook secret.

Both directions use the same timestamped signature, described below.

## Settlement instructions

SmartRemit sends a `POST` to your settlement endpoint:

```http
POST https://partner.example.com/settlements
x-smartremit-signature: t=1790000000,v1=5d2e…
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "reference": "tr_abc123",
  "partner_id": "acme",
  "corridor": { "source": "US", "destination": "IN" },
  "payout":   { "rail": "bank", "destination": "123456789012|HDFC0001234" },
  "recipient":{ "name": "Anita Sharma", "phone": "+12025550143" },
  "amount": {
    "source": 200, "currency": "USD",
    "destination": 16600, "destination_currency": "INR",
    "fx_rate": 83
  }
}
```

- `reference` is our transfer ID. Echo it in your status callbacks.
- The instruction is **at least once**: deduplicate on `reference`, so a retry can never pay out twice.
- Answer `2xx`, optionally with a JSON body carrying `providerRef` (your reference, stored once against the transfer).
- When you sent a `client_reference` and your own rail pays the transfer out, the instruction carries it as `client_reference`. A transfer paid out by another partner's rail never carries it.

### Compliance block

Instructions also carry an additive `compliance` object. Ignore unknown keys, and do not reject an instruction that lacks it.

```json
"compliance": {
  "version": 1,
  "originator": {
    "entity_type": "individual",
    "name": "Ravi Kumar", "country": "US", "phone": "12025550142",
    "id_type": "passport", "id_last4": "1234"
  },
  "beneficiary": { "entity_type": "individual", "name": "Anita Sharma",
                   "relationship": "parent", "country": "IN" },
  "purpose": "family_support",
  "purpose_code": null,
  "kyc": { "status": "verified", "tier": "T1", "verified_at": "2026-09-01T12:00:00.000Z" },
  "screening": { "status": "cleared", "reasons": [],
                 "screened_at": "2026-09-23T10:00:00.000Z",
                 "list_source": "…", "list_version": "…", "decision": "clear" },
  "edd_required": false
}
```

- `version` is `1`. `originator` has `entity_type` (`individual` or `business`; for a business, `name` is the business), `name`, `country`, `phone`, `id_type` and `id_last4`.
- Never sent: the full ID number, date of birth, email or residential address. The originator's address is not included yet.
- `purpose` is our purpose value. `purpose_code` is always `null` for now.
- `screening.list_source`, `list_version` and `decision` appear only when a sanctions screening record exists for the transfer.
- A transfer settled on another partner's rail carries `"routed": true` and `originator: null`: the sender's identity is not shared with another partner. `originator` is also `null` when it could not be loaded, and older instructions have no block.
- The signatures cover the block like every other byte of the body.

### Reversals

A cancelled transfer whose funds your rail pulled gets a signed **reverse** instruction. It has `"action": "reverse"` and its own `reference`, distinct from the original instruction's, so your rail can tell a reverse from a replay. A reverse for a debit that never happened must be a no-op on your side.

### Ack deadline

We wait at most <Fact name="railAckTimeoutSec" /> seconds for your `2xx`. A slower answer is treated as a failure and the same instruction is retried. Persist and acknowledge first, then process asynchronously.

### Endpoint rules

- A public `https://` host on port 443. No IP addresses, no internal or single-label host names, no credentials in the URL.
- We follow at most <Fact name="maxRedirects" /> redirects, and only `307` or `308` to the same origin. A `301`, `302` or `303` would turn the signed `POST` into a `GET`, so it is refused.
- Your acknowledgement must be <Fact name="ackMaxKb" /> KB or less, uncompressed: we send `Accept-Encoding: identity` and never decompress. `providerRef` is at most <Fact name="providerRefMaxChars" /> characters of letters, digits and `. _ : -`.

An endpoint that breaks these rules is refused before the instruction is sent; the instruction is retried, so fix the endpoint on your dashboard (Integrations → Settlement webhooks) and the retries pick it up.

## Retries

Instructions are delivered at least once. A failed attempt is retried with exponential backoff: roughly `2^n` seconds after the n-th attempt, capped at <Fact name="instructBackoffCapSec" /> seconds between attempts. After <Fact name="instructMaxAttempts" /> attempts (about a day in total) the instruction stops retrying and SmartRemit's operations team is alerted to resolve it with you.

## Status callbacks

Report each transfer's status to the callback URL shown on your dashboard's partner page (it has the shape `https://smartremit.ai/api/payment-webhook/{provider}`), signed with your webhook secret:

```http
POST https://smartremit.ai/api/payment-webhook/acme-rail
x-smartremit-signature: t=1790000000,v1=9c44…
Content-Type: application/json

{ "reference": "tr_abc123", "status": "paid_out",
  "amount": { "destination": 16600, "destination_currency": "INR" } }
```

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `created` | Awaiting payment (no change). |
| `funded` | Paid: the customer was charged on your side. |
| `paid_out` | Delivered. Triggers the delivery notifications. |
| `failed` | Cancelled. If SmartRemit captured the charge it is refunded; a partner-pulled debit gets a signed `reverse`; otherwise you return the funds on your side. The customer is told. |
| `returned` | Cancelled, as for `failed`: if SmartRemit captured the charge it is refunded; a partner-pulled debit gets a signed `reverse`; otherwise you return the funds on your side. The customer is told. |

- The state machine only moves forward, so replays and out-of-order callbacks are safe.
- A `failed` or `returned` may carry an optional `reason` string. It is stored on the transfer for your operations team and ours, cut to <Fact name="failureReasonMaxChars" /> characters.
- A `failed` after `paid_out` is recorded for operations and never reverses a delivery. A `paid_out` after `failed` is refused and the transfer stays cancelled.
- Send your payout confirmation (for example a bank UTR) as `payout_reference` on `paid_out`: 1 to 64 letters, digits and `. _ : / # -`. We save the first one; later ones, and a value that does not fit, are ignored, and the status still applies.
- **Echo the amount on `paid_out`.** Send back the `amount.destination` and `destination_currency` from our instruction. If they differ, the transfer is not marked delivered: it is held for our operations team, who resolve it with you, and we answer with `"held": true` so you stop retrying. A `paid_out` with no `amount` is accepted for now but is deprecated.
- Unsigned, mis-signed and out-of-window callbacks are rejected with `401`.

## Signatures

Every instruction and every callback carries:

```text
x-smartremit-signature: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex>
v1 = hex( HMAC-SHA256( secret, t + "." + rawBody ) )
```

Use your **signing secret** to verify our instructions, and your **webhook secret** to sign your callbacks. To verify:

```text
signed   = t + "." + rawBody          (the exact bytes received)
expected = hex(HMAC_SHA256(secret, signed))
valid    = |now - t| within the tolerance
           AND some v1 in the header equals expected (constant-time compare)
```

- The tolerance is <Fact name="signatureToleranceSec" /> seconds either side of now. Reject anything outside it.
- Compare in constant time.
- Keep a record of the messages you have processed (for example a hash of `t.rawBody`) for at least 15 minutes, and answer a repeat with `2xx` without acting on it. We do the same: a repeated callback gets `{ "ok": true, "duplicate": true }`.
- **Secret rotation.** When a secret is changed, the old one stays valid for <Fact name="secretRotationOverlapDays" /> days. During that time our instructions carry one `v1` per active secret: accept the message if any of them matches. Your callbacks may be signed with either secret.
- **Deprecated:** the older `x-signature` header (an HMAC of the raw body with no timestamp) is still sent, and still accepted when `x-smartremit-signature` is absent. It will be retired, so verify the timestamped header now. When both are present, `x-smartremit-signature` alone decides.
