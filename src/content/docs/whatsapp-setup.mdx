## Bring your own number

Your customers can chat with the assistant on your own WhatsApp Business number, under your brand.

1. In your dashboard, enter your number's phone-number ID, access token, verify token and app secret.
2. Point your Meta app's webhook at the callback URL shown on your dashboard's partner page, using the verify token you entered.
3. Send your number a message. Inbound messages on your number are routed to your account, and replies, sign-in codes and delivery notices leave from your number under your brand.

Meta's own setup steps are in the [WhatsApp Business Platform documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/).

## Message templates

WhatsApp only lets a business start a conversation, or write to someone who has not messaged it in the last 24 hours, with a **message template** that Meta has approved. Create each template in [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/) for your own WhatsApp Business account, **exactly** as listed below: the same name, the same category and the language **English** (code `en`, not `en_US`). A template approved under a different name or language fails, and the message falls back to plain text, which only reaches someone who messaged your number in the last 24 hours.

## Submission order

If Meta decides a template submitted as Utility is really marketing, it can re-categorise it without notice and block new Utility templates on your whole WhatsApp Business account for 7 days. To keep the risk low:

1. Submit the authentication template and the plain transactional ones first.
2. Then the templates with a URL button.
3. Submit reminder-style templates last.

Keep every template strictly transactional, with no promotional wording.
